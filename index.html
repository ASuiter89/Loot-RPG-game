How to Play
Goal
Delve floor by floor, grab ever-better loot, and survive as deep as you can. Death sends you back to Town, lighter — but your hero lives on.
Moving & fighting
MoveArrow keys / WASD, or click the map — the hero routes around walls to get there; hold to keep going, diagonals included.
SprintHold Shift (or set Sprint to TOGGLE in Settings to auto-sprint).
DashSpace. Sprint & Dash burn stamina, which refills as you ease off.
AttackAutomatic on any foe in reach.
SkillsSkill bar or number keys (cost MP).
UseE — grab loot, talk to NPCs.
Mind your footing — ice slips, mud slows, arrow traps & fire vents can be dodged. Foes roam and give chase when you're close.
Loot rarity
Colour is rarity: grey → white → green → blue → purple → orange → red (Mythic). Click loot in your bag to equip, sell, or scrap it.
Teal marks a set piece — its own top-rarity class, as rare as red. Wear 2 or 4 matching pieces for escalating bonuses; completing a set unlocks a unique power and a golden aura. Hover a "… set" tag to see them.
Skills & potions
Skills cost MP and each has its own cooldown in seconds; their buttons glow gold when ready. Health & mana potions share a brief cooldown and pulse when ready to drink.
Hazards
Lava and spikes hurt more the deeper you go — step around them (you're never forced through one).
Stairs & Town
Clear every foe to unseal the glowing down-stairs (the up-stairs just backtrack). In Town, visit the Healer, Merchant, Ramen House and more, then take the Dungeon Gate back down.
History
Auto-Loot
When loot drops, instantly Scrap it into crafting materials or Sell it for gold by rarity, so your bag doesn't fill with junk. Default is Keep (off). Locked gear is never touched, and you can still Sell or Scrap by hand from the LOOT bag at any time.
