How to Play
Goal
Delve floor by floor, grab ever-better loot, and survive as deep as you can. Death sends you back to Town, lighter — but your hero lives on.
Moving & fighting
MoveArrow keys / WASD, or click the map — the hero routes around walls to get there; hold to keep going, diagonals included.
SprintHold Shift (or set Sprint to TOGGLE in Settings to auto-sprint).
DashSpace. Sprint & Dash burn stamina, which refills as you ease off.
AttackAutomatic on any foe in reach.
SkillsSkill bar or number keys (cost MP).
UseE — grab loot, talk to NPCs.
Loot rarity
Colour is rarity: grey → white → green → blue → purple → orange → red (Mythic). Click loot in your bag to equip, sell, or scrap it.
Skills & potions
Skills cost MP and each has its own cooldown in seconds; their buttons glow gold when ready. Health & mana potions share a brief cooldown and pulse when ready to drink.
Going deeper
Clear every foe to unseal the glowing down-stairs and drop to the next floor.
